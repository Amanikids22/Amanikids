PATHWAY 02 • SERVE

Share Your Time, Skills & Presence

Volunteering at Amani Kids is not transactional charity; it is an authentic human relationship. You will join an intergenerational family of mentors, cultural leaders, and community members dedicated to youth potential.

"To mentor a newcomer child is to offer them the most precious gift of all: the knowledge that they belong in Charlotte."

PATHWAY 03 • COLLABORATE

Institutional Partnerships

We actively partner with Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools, healthcare clinics, cultural institutions, corporate sponsors, and civic coalitions to create a seamless continuum of care for newcomer families.

Connecting African refugee, immigrant, and newcomer families with trusted resources, cultural belonging, and educational support to help them thrive in a new home.
