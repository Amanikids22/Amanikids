FAMILY WELCOME PORTAL

Looking for support? You are not alone.

Whether your family arrived in Charlotte last week or several years ago, Amani Kids is here to walk beside you with patience, dignity, and native language understanding.

English: You are welcome here. • Kiswahili: Karibu sana, tuko hapa kukusaidia. • Lingala: Boyei malamu, tozali elongo na bino. • Français: Bienvenue, nous sommes là pour vous guider.
WHAT TO EXPECT

How Amani Kids Walks With You

We know that reaching out for help can feel overwhelming. At Amani Kids, you will find a warm, welcoming community, complete confidentiality, and 100% free support.

1

We Listen in Your Language

A trusted cultural coordinator speaks with you by phone or in person in the language you feel most comfortable using.

2

Direct Resource Connection

We accompany you to school registration, match children with after-school tutoring, or connect you with healthcare clinics.

3

An Ongoing Community Safety Net

You are invited to Bisakana Day festivals, family circles, and social networks where lifelong friendships take root.

Prefer to Call Directly?

You are always welcome to call our Charlotte office directly to speak with a community advocate.
