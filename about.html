WHO WE ARE

Rooted in Peace.

Amani Kids is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit built from lived experience to ensure that African refugee, immigrant, and newcomer families arriving in Charlotte find understanding, belonging, and the tools to flourish.

OUR MISSION

Why We Exist

Connect children and families of the African Diaspora, who are refugees, immigrants, and newcomers, to resources, opportunities and foster their wellbeing to thrive.

OUR VISION

The Future We Build

A welcoming community where newcomer African families are never isolated by language or culture; where children celebrate their dual heritage with pride; and where every immigrant parent has equal access to opportunity, education, and peace.

Binti Muzuri Amisi

FOUNDER & EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR

FOUNDER'S STORY

"I lived this journey. That is why Amani Kids exists."

Binti Muzuri Amisi founded Amani Kids not out of an academic exercise in social work, but out of the raw reality of arriving in the United States from the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Like thousands of newcomers, she found that the transition was filled with unexpected friction: forms written only in English, school enrollment systems with no Swahili or Lingala translations, medical appointments where vital questions went unasked, and an aching absence of cultural community.

She observed brilliant African children struggling in classrooms simply because language bridges were absent, and hard-working parents feeling disempowered. She resolved to create the organization she wished had existed when she arrived: a trusted, compassionate bridge that honors the dignity and cultural richness of the African diaspora.

"Amani means Peace in Swahili. When you take away the fear of the unknown, families can finally exhale, heal, and begin to flourish."

— Binti Muzuri Amisi
GUIDING ETHICS

The Eight Anchors of Amani Kids

Our principles are woven into every program, volunteer training, and family conversation.

01 • AMANI

Peace

Cultivating emotional safety, restful environments, and peaceful transitions for families who have lived through displacement.

02 • UPENDO

Love

Approaching every child and caregiver with deep kindness, patience, and unwavering personal respect.

03 • BISAKANA

Joy

Celebrating through dance, play, food, and laughter as powerful vehicles for healing, resilience, and connection.

04 • HESHIMA

Respect

Honoring the traditions, wisdom, resilience, and unique cultural backgrounds of African newcomer families.

05 • UMOJA

Diversity

Celebrating the multi-ethnic, multilingual richness of Africa without flattening distinct cultures into a monolith.

06 • KUKARIBISHA

Inclusion

Ensuring an open table where all newcomers have a seat, a voice, and an advocate by their side.

07 • USAWA

Equity

Striving to ensure every child and family has fair access to educational opportunities, resources, and community representation.

08 • USHIRIKIANO

Collaboration

We believe stronger communities are built by working together with families, partners, and volunteers.
